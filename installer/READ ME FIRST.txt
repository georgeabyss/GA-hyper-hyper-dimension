Hyper! Hyper! Dimension - how to install
========================================

This installer is not signed with a paid Apple developer certificate, so macOS
will not open it with a plain double-click the first time. That is expected.

macOS 15 (Sequoia) and newer:
  1. Double-click the .pkg. macOS says it cannot be opened. Click "Done".
  2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security and scroll down.
     Next to "Hyper Hyper Dimension ... was blocked", click "Open Anyway".
  3. Confirm with your password. The installer starts.

macOS 11 to 14:
  Right-click (or Ctrl-click) the .pkg, choose "Open", then "Open" again.

Then follow the installer. You can choose VST3 and / or Audio Unit.
Restart your DAW (or rescan plug-ins) afterwards.

Needs macOS 11 (Big Sur) or newer, Apple Silicon or Intel.
