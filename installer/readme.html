Where it goes

VST3: /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
Audio Unit: /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
Your own presets are saved in ~/Music/GA/Hyper! Hyper! Dimension/Presets.

After installing

Restart your DAW, or let it rescan its plug-ins. In Logic, the Audio Unit is under Audio Units > GA. Retrig needs MIDI routed to the plug-in (in Logic: the MIDI side-chain menu in the plug-in header).

Updating

Just run the new installer, it replaces the old version. Your presets stay.

Uninstalling

Delete Hyper! Hyper! Dimension.vst3 and Hyper! Hyper! Dimension.component from the folders above.
